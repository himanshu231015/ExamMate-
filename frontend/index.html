RGPV 4-Year B.Tech Curriculum & Subject Repository

Complete year-wise syllabus trees, units, exam weightages, and course modules.

BT-101
Engineering Chemistry Semester 1 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼

Subject Scope & Units:

UNIT I: Water & Analysis
Hardness determination by EDTA method, boiler troubles, water softening processes (Zeolite & Demineralization).
UNIT II: Polymers & Lubricants
Classification of polymers, Bakelite, Nylon 6:6, mechanism of lubrication, viscosity index, flash & fire point.
UNIT III: Corrosion & Control
Electrochemical corrosion mechanism, galvanic corrosion, sacrificial anode protection, paint coatings.
UNIT IV: Spectroscopy & Refractories
UV-Visible & IR Spectroscopy principles, Beer-Lambert Law, refractory materials characteristics.
UNIT V: Fuel & Combustion
Proximate & Ultimate coal analysis, calorific value determination (Bomb Calorimeter), numerical problems.
BT-102
Mathematics - I Semester 1 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼
UNIT I: Calculus & Expansion
Maclaurin's & Taylor's theorem, indeterminate forms, curvature and asymptotes.
UNIT II: Partial Differentiation
Euler's theorem on homogeneous functions, Jacobians, maxima & minima of two variables.
UNIT III: Matrices & Linear Algebra
Rank of matrix, consistency of linear equations, Eigen values & vectors, Cayley-Hamilton theorem.
UNIT IV: First Order Differential Eqns
Exact differential equations, linear differential equations, Bernoulli's equation, orthogonal trajectories.
UNIT V: Higher Order ODEs
Linear differential equations with constant coefficients, Cauchy-Euler equations, method of variation of parameters.
BT-201
Engineering Physics Semester 2 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼
UNIT I: Quantum Mechanics
Wave-particle duality, de-Broglie hypothesis, Heisenberg uncertainty principle, Schrödinger wave equation.
UNIT II: Wave Optics
Interference in thin films, Newton's rings experiment, Fraunhofer diffraction, diffraction grating.
UNIT III: Lasers & Holography
Spontaneous & stimulated emission, population inversion, Ruby Laser, He-Ne Laser, industrial laser applications.
UNIT IV: Fiber Optics
Total internal reflection, acceptance angle, numerical aperture, step-index & graded-index fiber modes.
UNIT V: Superconductivity & Nanomaterials
Meissner effect, Type-I & Type-II superconductors, synthesis and carbon nanotubes (CNTs).
CS-301
Data Structures Semester 3 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼
UNIT I: Arrays & Stacks ADT
Array representations, Stack LIFO operations, infix-to-postfix conversion, evaluation of postfix expressions, Circular queues.
UNIT II: Linked Lists
Singly, doubly, and circular linked lists, pointer manipulation, in-place list reversal, polynomial addition.
UNIT III: Trees & AVL Rotations
Binary Search Trees (BST), AVL Tree balancing factor, single & double rotations (LL, RR, LR, RL), Threaded trees.
UNIT IV: Graphs & Traversal
Adjacency matrix & list, BFS queue traversal, DFS recursive traversal, Dijkstra shortest path, Prim's and Kruskal's MST.
UNIT V: Sorting & Hashing
Quick Sort, Merge Sort recurrence, Heap Sort, Hash table collisions, linear probing and chaining techniques.
CS-402
Analysis & Design of Algorithms (ADA) Semester 4 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼
UNIT I: Asymptotic Analysis
Big-O, Omega, Theta notations, Master Theorem for recurrences, Divide and Conquer paradigms.
UNIT II: Greedy Strategy
Fractional Knapsack problem, Huffman coding, Job Sequencing with deadlines, Minimum Spanning Trees.
UNIT III: Dynamic Programming
0/1 Knapsack problem table, Matrix Chain Multiplication, Longest Common Subsequence (LCS), Floyd-Warshall.
UNIT IV: Backtracking & Branch-and-Bound
N-Queens problem, Graph Coloring algorithm, Hamiltonian cycle, Travelling Salesperson problem.
UNIT V: NP-Completeness & Matching
P, NP, NP-Complete and NP-Hard classes, Cook's theorem, KMP string matching, Rabin-Karp algorithm.
CS-403
Operating Systems Semester 4 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼
UNIT I: Process Management
Process control block (PCB), context switching, threads, system calls.
UNIT II: CPU Scheduling & Synchronization
FCFS, SJF, Round Robin scheduling, critical section problem, Semaphores, Peterson's solution.
UNIT III: Deadlocks
Deadlock conditions, Resource allocation graph, Banker's algorithm for avoidance, detection & recovery.
UNIT IV: Memory Management
Paging, Segmentation, Virtual memory, Demand paging, Page replacement algorithms (FIFO, LRU, Optimal).
UNIT V: Storage & File Systems
Disk scheduling (SSTF, SCAN, C-SCAN), File allocation methods, Directory structure.
CS-501
Theory of Computation (TOC) Semester 5 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼
UNIT I: Automata & Languages
DFA, NFA, epsilon-NFA, conversion from NFA to DFA, Minimization of DFA using table-filling.
UNIT II: Regular Languages
Regular expressions, Arden's theorem, Pumping Lemma for regular sets, closure properties.
UNIT III: Context-Free Grammars
Derivation trees, ambiguity, Chomsky Normal Form (CNF), Greibach Normal Form (GNF).
UNIT IV: Pushdown Automata (PDA)
Deterministic & Non-deterministic PDA, acceptance by empty stack and final state, equivalence with CFG.
UNIT V: Turing Machines & Computability
Turing machine model, Halting problem, Church-Turing thesis, Post Correspondence Problem (PCP).
CS-502
Database Management Systems (DBMS) Semester 5 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼
UNIT I: Data Models & ER Model
Three-tier architecture, ER diagrams, entity sets, relationship constraints, extended ER features.
UNIT II: Relational Algebra & SQL
Selection, projection, joins, SQL queries, DDL, DML, views, nested queries and triggers.
UNIT III: Database Normalization
Functional dependencies, 1NF, 2NF, 3NF, Boyce-Codd Normal Form (BCNF), multi-valued dependencies (4NF).
UNIT IV: Transaction & Concurrency
ACID properties, serializability, two-phase locking (2PL) protocol, timestamp ordering, deadlock handling.
UNIT V: Indexing & Storage
B-Trees, B+ Trees indexing structures, static and dynamic hashing, crash recovery logs.
CS-602
Computer Networks Semester 6 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼
UNIT I: Network Models
OSI 7-layer reference model, TCP/IP protocol suite, transmission media, switching techniques.
UNIT II: Data Link Layer
Framing, Error control (CRC, Checksum), Sliding Window protocols (Stop-and-Wait, Go-Back-N, Selective Repeat).
UNIT III: Network Layer
IPv4 addressing, subnetting, CIDR, Distance Vector Routing, Link State Routing (OSPF), BGP.
UNIT IV: Transport Layer
TCP 3-way handshake, flow control, congestion control algorithms (Leaky Bucket, Token Bucket), UDP.
UNIT V: Application Layer
DNS domain resolution, HTTP/HTTPS protocols, FTP, SMTP email transfer, Network security basics.
CS-701
Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Semester 7 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼
UNIT I: AI Search Strategies
State space search, BFS, DFS, Heuristic search, A* algorithm, AO* algorithm, Minimax game playing.
UNIT II: Knowledge Representation
Propositional logic, First-Order Predicate Logic (FOPL), resolution refutation, semantic networks, frames.
UNIT III: Supervised Learning
Linear regression, Logistic classification, Decision Trees (ID3), Support Vector Machines (SVM), Naive Bayes.
UNIT IV: Neural Networks & Deep Learning
Perceptron learning rule, Multilayer perceptron, Backpropagation gradient descent, CNN overview.
UNIT V: Unsupervised & Reinforcement
K-Means clustering, PCA dimensionality reduction, Q-Learning reward mechanisms, NLP tokenization.
CS-702
Cryptography & Information Security Semester 7 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼
UNIT I: Classical Cryptography
Symmetric key ciphers, DES, AES structure, Block cipher modes of operation (CBC, CTR).
UNIT II: Public Key Cryptography
Number theory, Fermat's Little Theorem, RSA algorithm, Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange, ECC.
UNIT III: Hash Functions & Signatures
SHA-256, MD5, Digital Signature Standard (DSS), HMAC message authentication codes.
UNIT IV: Network Security Protocols
IPsec architecture, SSL/TLS handshake, Kerberos authentication, PGP email security.
UNIT V: System Attacks & Defenses
Buffer overflow, SQL injection, Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS), Firewalls and Intrusion Detection (IDS).
CS-801
Cloud Computing & Distributed Systems Semester 8 • 4 Credits
5 Units Complete ▼
UNIT I: Cloud Delivery Models
IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, Public, Private & Hybrid clouds, Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
UNIT II: Virtualization Technologies
Hypervisors (Type 1 & Type 2), CPU and memory virtualization, containers vs virtual machines.
UNIT III: Distributed Storage & Compute
Google File System (GFS), Hadoop Distributed File System (HDFS), MapReduce programming model.
UNIT IV: Containers & Orchestration
Docker container lifecycle, Kubernetes architecture (Pods, Services, Deployments), microservices.
UNIT V: Cloud Security & Governance
Shared responsibility model, IAM policies, cloud data privacy, disaster recovery and backup.

DECEMBER 2023

Data Structures (CS-301) — Regular Examination

Total Marks: 70
Q1 (a) [7 Marks]

What is a Stack ADT? Explain the algorithm to convert an infix expression into a postfix expression using stack with a suitable example: (A + B * C) / (D - E).

Q1 (b) [7 Marks]

Explain the circular queue with its insertion and deletion algorithms. Why is a circular queue preferred over a linear queue?

Q2 (a) [7 Marks]

Write a C/C++ function to reverse a singly linked list in-place without using extra auxiliary memory.

Q3 (a) [14 Marks]

Construct an AVL tree by inserting the following key elements sequentially: 21, 26, 30, 9, 4, 14, 28, 18, 15, 10. Show every rotation step clearly.

Data Structures (CS-301) — Main Exam
